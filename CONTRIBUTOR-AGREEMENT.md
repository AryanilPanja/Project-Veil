# Project Veil Contributor Agreement (version 1)

> Project Veil is proprietary software (see [LICENSE](LICENSE)). This agreement is how someone
> other than the copyright holder may work on it. It is a plain-language template, not legal
> advice; have it checked if anything here matters to you.

This agreement is between **Aryanil Panja** (the "Owner", copyright holder of Project Veil) and
the person who signs below (the "Contributor").

## 1. What the Contributor may do

The Owner grants the Contributor a personal, non-exclusive, non-transferable, revocable,
royalty-free permission to **use, copy and modify** the Project Veil source code, **only** to:
- develop, test and submit Contributions to the Owner's repository
  (`github.com/AryanilPanja/Project-Veil`), and
- keep a fork of that repository on GitHub for that purpose.

Nothing else is permitted: no distribution, publication, sublicensing, commercial use, or use of the
code in other projects. Every other right stays with the Owner. The Owner may end this permission at
any time by written notice (an email or a GitHub message is enough); the Contributor may end it at
any time too. When it ends, the Contributor deletes their copies, except that GitHub's own copy of
pull requests already opened may remain. Sections 2–5 survive the end of this agreement.

## 2. What the Contributor gives the Owner

A **Contribution** is any code, documentation, test or other material the Contributor submits to
the Owner's repository (a pull request, a commit pushed to it, an issue or review comment with code
in it), from the date of signing on.

For each Contribution, the Contributor grants the Owner a **perpetual, worldwide, irrevocable,
royalty-free, non-exclusive** license to use, copy, modify, publish, distribute and sublicense it,
and to **license it under any terms the Owner chooses, including proprietary terms and any future
open-source license**. The Contributor also grants the Owner a license, on the same terms, to any
patent claims of theirs that a Contribution necessarily infringes, for that Contribution as
submitted or combined with Project Veil.

The Contributor keeps the copyright in their Contributions and may use their own Contributions
(but not the rest of Project Veil) elsewhere.

## 3. What the Contributor promises

- Each Contribution is their **original work**, or they clearly mark any part that isn't, with its
  source and license, and that license allows the grant in section 2.
- They have the right to make the grant (for example, an employer or university has no claim on
  the work, or has agreed to it).
- **No personal data:** a Contribution contains no real personal information, theirs or anyone
  else's: no real names, emails, paths, keys or persona files. Tests use the synthetic persona
  (Jane Doe → Alex Smith). This is the project's core rule.
- They won't run, or ask anyone to run, Project Veil against someone else's real persona to test a
  Contribution.

## 4. No obligations

The Owner doesn't have to accept, merge, use or credit any Contribution. Contributions are given
"as is", without warranty. Neither side owes the other money under this agreement.

## 5. Credit

Unless the Contributor asks otherwise, merged Contributions keep their author in the git history.
The Owner may credit Contributors in release notes.
